                                                                                        Despite insights gained by bulk DNA sequencing of cancer it remains chal-
                                                                                        lenging to resolve the admixture of normal and tumor cells, and/or of distinct
                                                                                        tumor subclones; high-throughput single-cell DNA sequencing circumvents
                                                                                        these and brings cancer genomic studies to higher resolution. However, its
                                                                                        application has been limited to liquid tumors or a small batch of solid tumors,
                                                                                        mainly because of the lack of a scalable workﬂow to process solid tumor
                                                                                        samples. Here we optimize a highly automated nuclei extraction workﬂow that
                                                                                        achieves fast and reliable targeted single-nucleus DNA library preparation of
                                                                                        38 samples from 16 pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma patients, with an
                                                                                        average library yield per sample of 2867 single nuclei. We demonstrate that
                                                                                        this workﬂow not only performs well using low cellularity or low tumor purity
                                                                                        samples but reveals genomic evolution patterns of pancreatic ductal adeno-
                                                                                        carcinoma as well.
