Jónsson H, Magnusdottir E, Eggertsson HP, et al. **Differences between germline genomes of monozygotic twins.** *Nature Genetics* 53, 27–34 (2021). DOI：[10.1038/s41588-020-00755-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-020-00755-1)。PMID 33414551；沒有 PMCID。同儕審查。讀取層級：摘要（全文在付費牆後，PMC 沒收錄）。查核日期：2026-11-08。

## 實際讀到的證據

- **雙胞胎差異：** "monozygotic twins differ on average by 5.2 early developmental mutations and that approximately 15% of monozygotic twins have a substantial number of these early developmental mutations specific to one of them."（Abstract）〔論文〕
- **用家族找 pre-twinning 突變：** "Using the parents and offspring of twins, we identified pre-twinning mutations."（Abstract）〔論文〕
- **細胞分配：** "We observed instances where a twin was formed from a single cell lineage in the pre-twinning cell mass and instances where a twin was formed from several cell lineages."（Abstract）〔論文〕
- **突變類型隨發育變化：** "CpG>TpG mutations increased in frequency with embryonic development, coinciding with an increase in DNA methylation."（Abstract）〔論文〕
- **全文有相關圖表：** 公開頁面列出 "Fig. 3: Timing of pre-PGCS and pre-twinning mutations in twins"、"Fig. 5: The three-generation approach"、"Fig. 6: Mutation classes of pre-PGCS versus trio mutations"。圖的內容沒讀到。〔論文〕
- **父源 272、母源 310、P = 0.12：** 未核對。本檔另算 272/582 = 46.7%，雙尾 exact binomial p ≈ 0.13，和引述的 P = 0.12 接近；但這只檢查數字是否自洽，不代表原文這樣寫〔推論〕。
